The Research Proposal
Sarah stared at her blank computer screen, wondering how to begin her research proposal. The deadline was approaching, and she felt a growing sense of anxiety.
Her academic advisor had been very patient, offering guidance throughout the semester. But Sarah knew she needed to demonstrate her ability to work independently.
She opened her notebook and began to analyze the data she had collected over the past three months. The evidence was sufficient, but organizing it into a coherent argument was the real challenge.
Two hours later, Sarah leaned back in her chair with a sense of accomplishment. The proposal was finally taking shape — clear, well-structured, and persuasive. She clicked "submit" and smiled. [读完→完成]
